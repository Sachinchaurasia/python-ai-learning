✅ Missing steps

1. Tokenization

Converts the original text into smaller pieces called tokens.

"Java is powerful."
        ↓
"Java" | " is" | " powerful" | "."

2. Token IDs

Each token is mapped to a numerical ID from the tokenizer's vocabulary.

"Java" | " is" | " powerful"
   ↓       ↓          ↓
 1527     318        4921

The numbers above are only examples.
