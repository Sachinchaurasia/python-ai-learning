🧠 TASK 3 — Thinking Challenge

For the sentence:

"The dog chased the cat because it was fast."

When processing "it", the model should investigate the earlier words that could tell it what "it" refers to.

🔍 Important words

The strongest candidates are:

dog 🐕
cat 🐈
chased
fast

The model needs to determine whether "it" refers to the dog or the cat based on the context.

🧠 Why is attention useful?

Attention allows the token "it" to look at other tokens in the sentence and assign different importance to them.

Conceptually:

The   dog   chased   the   cat   because   it   was   fast
       ↑                              ↑
       │                              │
       └──── possible relationship ───┘
                                      "it"

The model might learn relationships such as:

"it" ↔ dog    → some attention
"it" ↔ cat    → some attention
"it" ↔ fast   → useful contextual information

The important point is that attention doesn't simply look at the nearest word. It considers relationships between tokens across the sequence.

🔥 Key idea

Attention is useful because it lets the model examine the surrounding context and determine which earlier words are important for understanding what "it" means.

⚠️ Also notice something interesting: this sentence is actually ambiguous. Without additional context, both dog and cat could potentially be the thing that was fast. A good model should recognize that ambiguity rather than assuming there is always one certain answer.
