🧠 TASK 3 — Thinking Challenge

For the sentence:

"The bank was crowded because many people wanted to deposit money."

The word "bank" should have a strong relationship with:

people
deposit
money
crowded
🧠 Why?

The word "deposit" is especially important because it gives strong context that "bank" means a financial institution, rather than a river bank.

For example:

"The bank was crowded because many people wanted to deposit money."
                          ↑
                       "bank"
                          ↓
             ┌────────────┼────────────┐
             ↓            ↓            ↓
          people       deposit       money
             \            |            /
              └──── context ──────────┘
🔥 Why is attention useful?

Attention allows the Transformer to look at the other words in the sentence and determine which ones are important for understanding "bank".

Without context:

bank

could mean:

🏦 financial institution
🌊 river bank

With attention, the model sees:

deposit + money + people

and can infer:

"bank" → financial institution

✅ In one sentence

Attention is useful because it allows the model to connect "bank" with contextual words such as "deposit" and "money", helping it understand the correct meaning of "bank".

Excellent example of self-attention: the meaning of one token depends on its relationship with other tokens in the sentence.
