🔹 In AI/ML

Vectors are useful because computers and neural networks can perform mathematical operations on them.

For example:

"Python"
   ↓
Embedding
   ↓
[0.21, -0.45, 0.78, 0.13]

The numbers themselves don't have an obvious human meaning individually. Together, they form a representation learned by the model.

🔥 Vector vs Embedding

This distinction is important:

Vector → A general mathematical list of numbers.

[2, 5, 8]

Embedding → A vector specifically used to represent something such as a word, token, sentence, image, etc., in a learned numerical space.

"Python"
   ↓
[0.21, -0.45, 0.78, ...]

So:

Every embedding is a vector, but not every vector is an embedding.

⭐ Easy analogy

Think of a vector like a coordinate.

On a simple map:

Point A = (2, 3)

has two numbers describing its location.

Similarly, an embedding might have hundreds or thousands of numerical dimensions describing a learned representation.

Vector = an ordered collection of numbers.
Embedding = a learned vector representing something.
